About Me

Turning Data into Business Value

I am a Business Information Systems student at the Arab Academy for Science, Technology & Maritime Transport (AAST), pursuing a dual degree in collaboration with the University of Northampton (UK), with a strong passion for data analytics, artificial intelligence, and business intelligence. Throughout my academic journey, I have developed a solid foundation in working with data—from database design and SQL querying to building interactive dashboards and backend systems that support data-driven decision-making.

I have hands-on experience with tools and technologies such as Python, Power BI, SQL, and Microsoft Office Suite, and I continuously expand my skill set through practical projects and specialized courses in data analysis, machine learning, and web scraping. I focus on building end-to-end solutions that combine data processing, analysis, and visualization to extract meaningful insights from real-world datasets.

Beyond technical skills, I bring strong communication, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities, which I have developed through academic projects and collaborative work. I am particularly interested in applying data to solve business problems—whether through identifying trends, optimizing processes, or supporting strategic decision-making.

My goal is to become a data analyst and evolve into an AI and machine learning engineer, building intelligent systems that create real business value. I am currently seeking internship opportunities in data analytics, business intelligence, or data science, where I can contribute, learn, and grow in a professional environment.
